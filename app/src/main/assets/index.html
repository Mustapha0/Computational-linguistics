📚 Scholarly starting points

Jurafsky, Daniel & James H. Martin — Speech and Language Processing
Online draft and chapters
Broad introduction to NLP methods, tasks, and evaluation. Cite the specific version/chapter consulted.

Pollard, Carl & Ivan A. Sag (1994) — Head-Driven Phrase Structure Grammar
Foundational monograph for HPSG; consult the original publication for formal definitions.

Chomsky, Noam (1957) — Syntactic Structures
Historically important work in generative syntax; read in its historical context rather than as a summary of all later generative theories.

de Marneffe, Marie-Catherine & Christopher D. Manning (2008) — “The Stanford Typed Dependencies Representation”
Paper
A specific dependency representation; modern annotation schemes may differ.

Universal Dependencies
Annotation guidelines and treebanks
Consult the documentation for the exact language treebank and version used.

Association for Computational Linguistics Anthology
Research papers
Use peer-reviewed papers for current claims; record publication year and evaluation setup.

Meaning–Text Theory
EUR ALEX resources and publications portal
Use a primary MTT publication for formal terminology and lexical functions; verify the exact source rather than relying on general summaries.

🧪 How to read research critically

  • Identify the research question and the claim being tested.
  • Check the dataset, language variety, annotation scheme, and license.
  • Compare against an appropriate baseline and inspect error categories.
  • Do not compare scores across incompatible datasets or metrics.
  • Distinguish a benchmark score from evidence of broad real-world capability.

External links require an internet connection. Bibliographic details and versions should be verified before academic citation.
